# Greatest Common Factor Examples

The Greatest Common Factor (GCF) is the largest positive number that divides two or more numbers without leaving a remainder.

## Example 1

Find the GCF of 12 and 18.

Factors of 12:

```text
1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12
```

Factors of 18:

```text
1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18
```

The greatest factor they share is:

```text
6
```

So the GCF is 6.

## Example 2: Factoring an Expression

Consider:

```text
8x + 12
```

The GCF of 8 and 12 is 4. Therefore:

```text
8x + 12 = 4(2x + 3)
```

You can verify the result by multiplying 4 through the parentheses.

## Quick Tip

When factoring an algebraic expression, checking for a GCF first is often a useful starting point.
