# Prime Factorization

Prime factorization means writing a whole number as a product of prime numbers.

## Example

Take the number 60.

We can divide it by 2:

```text
60 = 2 × 30
```

Continue factoring:

```text
30 = 2 × 15
15 = 3 × 5
```

Therefore:

```text
60 = 2 × 2 × 3 × 5
```

Using exponents:

```text
60 = 2² × 3 × 5
```

## Why Prime Factorization Is Useful

Prime factorization can help with:

- Finding the Greatest Common Factor
- Finding the Least Common Multiple
- Simplifying fractions
- Understanding number relationships
